1. **Use aggregators for discovery:** Use Google Flights or Skyscanner to compare schedules, prices, and routing options.
2. **Always book directly with the airline:** Avoid third-party Online Travel Agencies (OTAs) like Kiwi, eDreams, or Expedia. If there are cancellations, schedule changes, or delays, airlines often refuse to support OTA bookings directly, leaving you stranded with poor OTA customer service.
3. **Consider direct flights from neighboring hubs:** There are no direct flights from Brussels (BRU) to Taipei (TPE). Instead of booking a connecting flight from BRU (e.g., via Emirates or KLM), it is often more efficient to take a high-speed train to Amsterdam (AMS) or Paris (CDG) and catch a direct flight to TPE via EVA Air or China Airlines.
